## Église du Christ du Léman

Geneva (Genève in French, Genf in German) is the largest and most visited city on Lake Geneva. It is located in the French speaking region of Switzerland. Geneva is famous as the seat of a large number of international organizations and companies. There are more than 500’000 people living in the area of Geneva.

<!-- more -->

Our church of Christ was founded in 1996. Since 2007, the church took the name of Eglise du Christ du Léman, which means the Church of Christ of the Leman Lake. Leman is the local name of Lake Geneva. Today the church has about 70 disciples.

Worship services are on Sunday morning at 10am at Starling Hotel Geneva, located route François-Peyrot 34, 1218 Le Grand-Saconnex, close to the International Airport of Geneva. A shuttle service is available from the airport to the hotel. Once every 3 Sunday, we meet in house churches at 5 different locations. Thus, before coming to a worship service, you are invited to check the agenda available on the website of the church in order to get to know the location of the worship service.

The main characteristic of the Church of Christ of Leman Lake is its family spirit inspired by the Word of God. When you come to this Church, you will meet people of all generations. More than a quarter of church members are born after 1990…

If you want to enjoy a great time with the family of God, you are more than welcome to join us.
