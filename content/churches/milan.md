## Chiesa di Cristo di Milano

Milan, or Milano as it is known in Italy, is world reknown for fashion, design, architecture and, of course, Italian cuisine! As the largest city in the north of Italy, Milan has become the economic, technological and financial center of the bel paese. With a metropolitan area population of more than 3 million, the Madonnina continues to grow each year in tourism and development as people from all over the world come to visit, to work, to study and to experience its beauty and charm.

<!-- more -->

La Chiesa di Cristo di Milano, or Milan Church of Christ, is a congregation that began with a small group of committed missionaries in 1991 and now has about 80 members that live all around the city and its surrounding suburbs. The dream of the Milan church is to be a light to all of Italy by its members committing themselves to the teachings of the Bible and to following Jesus in their daily lives. The Chiesa di Cristo di Milano looks forward to a future of mission plantings and gatherings of true worshipers all over Italy.

All who find themselves in Milan are invited to come worship God and meet the spiritual family that assembles each Sunday morning at 10:15 in Auditorium Lattuada, found in Corso di Porta Vigentina 15a. If you wish to contact the church or learn more about it, please visit the official website (ccmilano.net) where you will find information and contacts forms. Ti aspettiamo!
